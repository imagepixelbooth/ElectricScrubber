1-Year Limited Warranty

At ViralTrenz, we stand behind the quality of our products. This Limited Warranty applies to physical goods, specifically the Electric Cleaning Brush, purchased directly from ViralTrenz.

What does this warranty cover?

This Limited Warranty covers any defects in material or workmanship under normal use during the Warranty Period. During this period, ViralTrenz will repair or replace, at no charge, products or parts of a product that proves defective because of improper material or workmanship, under normal use and maintenance.

How long does the coverage last?

The Warranty Period for physical goods purchased from ViralTrenz is 365 days (1 year) from the date of purchase.

What does this warranty not cover?

This Limited Warranty does not cover any problem that is caused by:

  • Conditions, malfunctions, or damage not resulting from defects in material or workmanship.
  • Damage resulting from improper maintenance or misuse.
  • Theft or loss of the product.

What do you have to do?

To obtain warranty service, you must contact us at support@viraltrenz.net to determine the problem and the most appropriate solution for you.
